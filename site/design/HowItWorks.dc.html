HOW IT WORKS

The vault

Six Singapore rules and your roster, as plain markdown files on your own machine. Open it in Obsidian, diff it in git. The folder is the product.

The curator

Nobody starts it. Every day it re-reads the government pages to check the law still says what your vault claims. On a quiet day it costs nothing at all — the model is never even loaded.

THE DAILY SWEEP
1  Detect
Pure Python. Hashes only the region of each page around that rule’s own keywords. 0 tokens
2  Understand
Only for a page that actually moved. The model is given no tools at all — it describes, and cannot act.
3  Verify
Five gates, pure Python, re-checking every claim against the bytes we fetched.
Only then does anything get written — and the previous file is kept, so one click puts it back.
