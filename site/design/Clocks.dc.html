THE THREE CLOCKS

THREE WAYS IT BITES

Every obligation that lands on a small company arrives on one of three clocks. Ante tags every rule with which one.

LAW
The rule moved

Same company, new number. Someone changed a figure and no letter arrived.

S Pass floor
S$3,300 → S$3,600
GROWTH
You moved

Same rule, and you just grew into it. The threshold never changed; you crossed it.

Revenue crosses S$1m
GST becomes compulsory
RECURRING
A date arrived

Neither moved. A deadline derived from your own financial year end came round.

ACRA annual return
FYE + 7 months

A calendar app can only ever handle the third. The first two are where the money is — and they are structurally invisible to a tool that stores only the dates you typed in yourself.
