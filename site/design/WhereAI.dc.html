WHERE THE AI IS

TWO CALLS. BOTH FENCED.

Roughly 3,700 lines of code, and a model is invoked in exactly two of them. That is the design, not an omission.

Reading a government page

The model is handed the old page and today’s, and reports what moved. It has no tools — it cannot fetch, write, or act. Then Python re-checks every word of it.

Explaining it to you

Who is affected was already settled in Python before the model speaks. Its job is to explain and prioritise — never to decide who is on the list.
