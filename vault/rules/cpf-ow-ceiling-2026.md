# What changes

The Ordinary Wage ceiling — the maximum monthly salary on which CPF contributions are
payable — rises from **S$7,400 to S$8,000**. This is the **final step** of an increase
schedule that ran in four stages from 1 September 2023.

Unchanged: the Additional Wage ceiling remains S$102,000 minus total Ordinary Wage subject
to CPF for the year, and the CPF Annual Limit remains **S$37,740**. The annual salary
ceiling stays at **S$102,000**.

# Who it hits

Only employees earning **above S$7,400 a month** who are CPF-liable (citizens and PRs).
Staff below that ceiling are unaffected. Foreign pass holders do not attract CPF.

# Cost

For each affected employee, an extra S$600 of monthly wage becomes CPF-liable. At the
17% employer rate for staff aged 55 and below, that is up to **S$102 a month, or
S$1,224 a year, per affected employee**.

# Next step

Count how many CPF-liable staff earn above S$7,400. Multiply by up to S$1,224 and add it
to the payroll budget. This one is already in force — if it has not been reflected, the
payroll figures being used for planning are understated.
