# What changes

The S Pass minimum qualifying salary rises. **It is not a flat figure — it increases
progressively with the holder's age from age 23.**

| | Now (entry, age 23 and below) | Rises with age to (45+) |
|---|---|---|
| All sectors except financial services | S$3,300 | S$4,800 |
| **From 1 Jan 2027** | **S$3,600** | **S$5,100** |
| Financial services | S$3,800 | S$5,650 |
| **Financial services from 1 Jan 2027** | **S$4,000** | **S$5,650** |

The new rates apply to **new applications submitted from 1 Jan 2027**, and to
**renewals of passes expiring from 1 Jan 2028**.

# Who it hits

Any S Pass holder paid near the current floor, especially one whose pass expires on or
after 1 Jan 2028. Because the requirement scales with age, an older holder needs
materially more than the entry figure — check the age-banded table on the MOM page for
the exact number, do not assume the entry rate.

# Cost

The gap between current salary and the applicable age-banded floor, times 12, plus
employer CPF where the holder is CPF-liable. A holder at S$3,400 facing a S$3,600 floor
costs an extra S$2,400 a year at the entry band alone.

If the salary is not raised, **the renewal fails and the worker cannot continue.**

# Next step

List every S Pass holder with their pass expiry date and current salary. For anyone whose
pass expires on or after 1 Jan 2028, look up their exact age-banded requirement on the
MOM page and calculate the gap now. Decide well before renewal whether to raise the
salary, restructure the role, or hire locally — a failed renewal has no remedy.
