# What changes

For wages earned from 1 January 2027, on monthly wages above S$750:

| Age band | Employer 2026 | Employer 2027 | Employee 2026 | Employee 2027 | Total 2026 | Total 2027 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 55 and below | 17% | 17% | 20% | 20% | 37% | 37% |
| **Above 55 to 60** | **16%** | **16.5%** | **18%** | **19%** | **34%** | **35.5%** |
| **Above 60 to 65** | **12.5%** | **13%** | **12.5%** | **13%** | **25%** | **26%** |
| Above 65 to 70 | 9% | 9% | 7.5% | 7.5% | 16.5% | 16.5% |
| Above 70 | 7.5% | 7.5% | 5% | 5% | 12.5% | 12.5% |

The increased contributions for ages 55 to 65 go entirely to the Retirement Account up to
the Full Retirement Sum, with any overflow to the Ordinary Account.

# Who it hits

CPF-liable employees aged above 55 and up to 65. Both bands see the **employer** share rise
by 0.5 percentage points. Employees in the 55-60 band also take a 1 percentage point cut in
take-home pay, which is a conversation to have before it lands, not after.

# Cost

0.5% of monthly wage per affected employee on the employer side. For a staff member at
S$6,000 a month, that is S$30 a month or **S$360 a year**. Modest per head — it matters
when several staff sit in these bands, and it compounds with the 2026 OW ceiling rise.

# Next step

Count staff who will be above 55 on 1 Jan 2027 — including anyone crossing 55 during 2026.
Budget 0.5% of their wages. Separately, tell the 55-60 band that their take-home drops by
1% so it is not a surprise in the January payslip.
